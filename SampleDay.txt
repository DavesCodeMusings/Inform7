[
	An example of a non-player character (NPC) asking a simple yes/no question and changing their state based on the answer.
	
	Test with: "take bread" then answer "yes" or "no" to get a conditional response.
]

The bakery is a room.
The player is in the bakery.
The baker is a person in the bakery.
The baker can be befriended. The baker is not befriended.
The bread is a thing in the bakery.

Instead of taking the bread:
	say "'Want a sample?' asks the baker.";
	if the player consents:
		say "'You like it? I'm glad.'";
		now the baker is befriended;
	otherwise:
		say "'Maybe next time.'"
